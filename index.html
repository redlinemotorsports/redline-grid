WEATHER / STRATEGY DESK

What the conditions can mean

Guidance based on the weather readings available for the selected session.

CONTEXT, NOT PREDICTION
Rain readings

Recorded rain can reduce grip and increase the relevance of intermediate or wet tyres. A dry sample does not guarantee a dry track throughout the session.

Track vs air temperature

A warmer track can increase tyre-energy demands, but compound, setup, traffic and stint length also matter.

Wind and humidity

Wind can affect balance and braking references; humidity alone is not a reliable rain forecast.

These are general interpretation notes, not a tyre recommendation. If session weather data is missing, no conditions are inferred.
